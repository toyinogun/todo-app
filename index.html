To do

A to do list that stays on your device.

Three short chapters, then the list itself. Or skip ahead and Open the app.

One list.

To do is a single list in your browser. You add a task, tick it when it is done, change the words, or delete it. There is nothing else to learn.

It opens where you left it, with the list exactly as you left it.

Your list is saved in this browser and never leaves it.

No account, no server, nothing to sign up for. Close the tab and it is still here. Clear the browser and it is gone, because there is nowhere else it could be.

Nobody can read your list, because nothing about it ever leaves the device you are holding.
